Badges

Use small count and label indicators in headings, buttons, and interface corners, with contextual colours and pill or dot shapes.

In headings

Badges scale to the size of their parent heading, so they work at every heading level.

In buttons

Place a badge inside a button and pair the number with visually hidden text so screen readers announce what it counts.

Positioned

Combine .position-absolute, .top-0, .start-100, and .translate-middle to pin a badge to the corner of a button.

Background colours

Use the .text-bg-* utilities to pair a contextual background with a readable text colour, on spans or on links.
