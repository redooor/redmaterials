Colors & backgrounds

Bootstrap's colour utilities set text, background, border, and subtle emphasis colours without writing custom CSS.

Text colors

Use .text-* utilities for contextual text, and the body utilities for colours that follow the active light or dark mode.

Subtle variants

Subtle backgrounds and emphasis text are designed to work together and adapt to the active colour mode, with a matching subtle border.

Background opacity

Apply .bg-opacity-* to a coloured background to make it more or less translucent.

.bg-opacity-10
.bg-opacity-25
.bg-opacity-50
.bg-opacity-75
.bg-opacity-100

Text opacity

Apply .text-opacity-* to theme-aware text to reduce its opacity. Lower opacities also reduce contrast, so use them sparingly; 75% is the lowest value shown here that still meets AA on the page surfaces.

.text-opacity-100

.text-opacity-75
