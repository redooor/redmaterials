Alerts & badges

scss/alerts.scss restyles Bootstrap's alert with 2em padding, 2px corners and the Material palette for the primary, success, info, warning and danger variants; scss/badges.scss keeps Bootstrap's .badge and adds the neutral .badge-default. Both keep Bootstrap's markup and need no JavaScript, except the dismissible alert's data-bs-dismiss.

Compared with stock Bootstrap. Stock Bootstrap uses its own alert padding, corners and colours; Redmaterials uses 2em padding, 2px corners and the Material palette for the primary, success, info, warning and danger alerts, while .alert-secondary, .alert-light and .alert-dark already match stock. Stock Bootstrap also has no .badge-default; Redmaterials adds it for a grey fill. The dismiss behaviour and all text-bg-* and bg-* badge markup are Bootstrap 5.

Badges
