Buttons

Redmaterials keeps Bootstrap's button markup and adds Material variants: raised, flat, circle and a card action button. The base buttons need no data attribute or JavaScript; the optional ripple needs the Redmaterials script, described in the Ripples section.

Compared with stock Bootstrap. Stock Bootstrap has no shadow on .btn-raised, no colour change on .btn-flat and square corners instead of .btn-circle, and its contextual variants use Bootstrap's blue, grey and green rather than the Material palette; Redmaterials adds the raised shadow, the flat colour change and the circular shape, and uses the Material palette. The palette composition has no fill in stock Bootstrap because the .bg-{colour} utilities are Redmaterials-only. The markup and the size, outline and disabled classes are Bootstrap 5 throughout.

Raised buttons

Add .btn-raised to a .btn for a depth-1 shadow that deepens on hover, focus and press.

Flat buttons

Add .btn-flat for a borderless, background-free button whose label takes the contextual colour and whose hover adds a subtle fill.

Circle buttons

Add .btn-circle for a 56px round button, size it with .btn-lg or .btn-sm, put a .material-icons icon inside, and give every icon-only button an aria-label so it has an accessible name.

The same classes work on <a> elements; use <button> when the control performs an action rather than navigating.

Card action button

Put .card-action on a card and .btn-action on the button it should float over its bottom edge.

Project card

The action button is positioned against the bottom-right corner of the card.

Default, white and muted

.btn-default is the mode-aware neutral, .btn-white is always white and .btn-muted is the Material grey; pair the fixed light fills with .text-dark so they stay readable in dark mode.

Contextual variants

Use Bootstrap's eight contextual classes with the theme's Material colours; .btn-light, .btn-info and .btn-warning keep Bootstrap's dark label.

Outline buttons

Bootstrap's outline buttons keep a 1px border (the theme removes it from filled buttons) and fill with the contextual colour on hover.

Sizes

Use .btn-lg or .btn-sm on a button; both also resize the icon and diameter of .btn-circle.

Button groups

Wrap a row of buttons in .btn-group; add .btn-group-lg or .btn-group-sm to size the whole group, and use .btn-group-vertical to stack it. Give every group role="group" and an aria-label that names it.

Disabled state

Disable a <button> with the disabled attribute and an <a> with .disabled, aria-disabled="true" and tabindex="-1".

Disabled link

Ripple effect on buttons

Add .ripple-effect to animate ink from the click point; it needs dist/js/redmaterials.min.js (no jQuery) and is explained in full under Ripples.

Palette colours

There is no .btn-{colour} class for the 19 Material palette colours: compose .bg-{colour} with .btn-raised instead, and choose .text-dark or .text-white for the label because the fixed palette fill is the same in both colour modes.
