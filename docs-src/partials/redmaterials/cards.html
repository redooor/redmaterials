Cards

Redmaterials keeps Bootstrap's card markup and changes the header and footer wash: scss/cards.scss points --bs-card-cap-bg at the theme token --rdm-card-cap-bg (3% of the body colour in light mode, 10% black in dark mode), so a coloured card keeps its text contrast instead of being lightened. Cards can use the .shadow-depth-* scale and the .card-action / .btn-action pair. No data attribute or JavaScript is needed.

Compared with stock Bootstrap. Stock Bootstrap's header and footer use its own translucent body-colour wash (which lightens coloured cards in dark mode), the six depth classes have no effect, and .card-action / .btn-action do not exist, so the action button sits in the normal flow; Redmaterials points --bs-card-cap-bg at --rdm-card-cap-bg and adds the depth scale and the action pair. The card, header, footer, image and list-group markup is Bootstrap 5 throughout.

Headers and footers

The header and footer share the card surface with the theme wash; put .text-bg-primary (or a palette .bg-*) on the whole card to see the token keep the coloured header dark enough for white text in both modes.

Card action

Add .card-action to a positioned card header (or another relative container) and .btn-action to the button; the class lifts it 27px below the header and 1.5em from the right edge. Give the icon-only button an aria-label and hide the Material Icons glyph from assistive technology.
