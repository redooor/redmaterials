Tabs & pills

Redmaterials restyles Bootstrap's tabs and pills: tabs lose the bordered strip and use a 2px Material underline on hover and on the active link, while pills shrink to 2px corners and fill with the Material primary. Both use Bootstrap's bundle JavaScript for data-bs-toggle="tab" or data-bs-toggle="pill"; no Redmaterials script is needed.

Compared with stock Bootstrap. Stock Bootstrap uses the bordered tab strip with rounded active tabs, and its pills use a larger corner radius and default blue; Redmaterials removes the border for a 2px Material underline on hover and on the active link, and shrinks pills to 2px corners filled with the Material primary. The nav markup, panes and data attributes are Bootstrap 5 throughout.

Tabs with panes

Add .nav-tabs to the nav and give each trigger data-bs-toggle="tab" plus a data-bs-target pointing at its .tab-pane; the first link starts .active and its pane starts .show.active.

Home pane: the Material underline marks the selected tab, and the disabled tab cannot be reached.

Profile pane: shown after the Profile tab is selected.

Contact pane: shown after the Contact tab is selected.

Pills with panes

Swap .nav-tabs for .nav-pills and use data-bs-toggle="pill"; the active pill takes the Material primary fill and white label.

Home pane for the pill navigation.

Profile pane: shown after the Profile pill is selected.

Contact pane: shown after the Contact pill is selected.

Disabled link

Add .disabled and aria-disabled="true" to a .nav-link to remove it from the interaction; Redmaterials also sets a not-allowed cursor.
