Popovers & tooltips

Redmaterials keeps Bootstrap's popover and tooltip markup and restyles the generated elements in scss/popover.scss and scss/tooltip.scss: popovers get the translucent Material surface, 2px corners and the depth-2 shadow, while tooltips get the high-contrast tooltip token, 2px corners and full opacity. Bootstrap's bundle JavaScript creates both from data-bs-toggle="popover" and data-bs-toggle="tooltip"; docs-src/assets/js/docs.js initialises every trigger on the page, so the examples need no extra script.

Bootstrap's popovers open on click by default and tooltips on hover or focus, so the tooltip triggers below add data-bs-trigger="click" to make the interaction easy to repeat. Compared with stock Bootstrap. Stock Bootstrap's popover has an opaque light surface, 0.5rem corners and a Bootstrap border, and its tooltip has 0.875rem corners and 0.9 opacity; Redmaterials gives the popover the translucent Material surface, 2px corners and the depth-2 shadow, and the tooltip a high-contrast token, 2px corners and full opacity. All data-bs-* attributes and placement names are Bootstrap 5.

Popover: top

data-bs-placement="top" opens the popover above its trigger.

Popover: right

data-bs-placement="right" opens the popover to the right of its trigger.

Popover: bottom

data-bs-placement="bottom" opens the popover below its trigger.

Popover: left

data-bs-placement="left" opens the popover to the left of its trigger.

Tooltips

Tooltips use the same four Bootstrap placement values; each trigger opts into a click trigger so the tooltip stays open long enough to inspect.

Tooltip: top

data-bs-placement="top" shows the tooltip above its trigger.

Tooltip: right

data-bs-placement="right" shows the tooltip to the right of its trigger.

Tooltip: bottom

data-bs-placement="bottom" shows the tooltip below its trigger.

Tooltip: left

data-bs-placement="left" shows the tooltip to the left of its trigger.
