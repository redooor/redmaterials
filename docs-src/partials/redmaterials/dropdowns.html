Dropdowns

Redmaterials keeps Bootstrap's dropdown markup and restyles the menu: square corners, no border, the Material depth shadow and roomier items. The menu is opened by Bootstrap's bundle JavaScript through data-bs-toggle="dropdown"; no Redmaterials script or extra data attribute is needed. Add data-bs-theme="dark" to a .dropdown-menu for a dark menu.

Compared with stock Bootstrap. Stock Bootstrap's menu uses a 1px border, rounded corners, its lighter shadow and tighter item padding; Redmaterials removes the border, squares the corners, uses the Material depth shadow and roomier items. The markup, placement classes and data-bs-* options are Bootstrap 5 throughout.

Basic dropdown

Put .dropdown-toggle and data-bs-toggle="dropdown" on the trigger and a .dropdown-menu next to it; Redmaterials supplies the square, borderless menu and depth shadow.

Headers and states

Use .dropdown-header for a non-interactive label, .dropdown-divider to separate groups, .dropdown-item-text for plain text, and .active or .disabled on an item to show its state.

Split button and alignment

A split button keeps the main action separate from the menu trigger, and .dropdown-menu-end aligns the menu to the right edge of its trigger.
